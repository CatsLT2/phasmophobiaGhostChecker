Evidence Testing Procedures

EMF Level 5

Triggers on physical interactions, throw events, or window dabs (25% chance). Must register a solid 5 on the handheld meter.

Spirit Box

Must be in the ghost room/near ghost with lights OFF in the room. Ask: "Where are you?", "How old are you?", "Are you friendly?".

DOTS Projector

Ghost silhouette crosses green laser grid. Note: Goryo DOTS is only visible through a video camera when no players are in the room.

Freezing Temperatures

Requires thermometer reading below 0°C (32°F) or visible freeze breath. Breath can appear at slightly higher temps depending on update rules.

UV / Fingerprints

Check doors, light switches, windows, and salt footprints using UV light or Glowstick within 2 minutes of interaction.

Ghost Writing

Place book near active area. Ghost will write or throw it. If it throws without writing, rule out Ghost Writing (except if tricked).

Cursed Possessions Quick Reference

Tarot Cards

10 cards per deck. Death: Cursed Hunt. The Moon: 0% Sanity. Sun: 100% Sanity. The Fool: Fake card trick. The Hanged Man: Instant death.

Ouija Board

Ask: "Where is the bone?", "Where are you?", "What is my sanity?". Remember to say "Goodbye" to avoid breaking the board and triggering a hunt.

Haunted Mirror

Shows a view of the ghost room in real-time. Drains sanity rapidly (~10%/sec). Breaks when sanity reaches 0.

Music Box

Plays tune. Ghost sings along and walks toward box. If ghost touches player or walks 5+ seconds, initiates cursed hunt.
